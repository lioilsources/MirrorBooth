Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

MirrorBooth is a camera app that shows a real-time symmetric mirror of your face. We do not collect personal information. All image processing happens on your device, and your photos and videos never leave it unless you share them yourself. To learn which features are used and where the app fails, it sends anonymous usage statistics, described below, which you can turn off at any time.

Camera

MirrorBooth uses your device's camera to render the live mirror preview. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded, analyzed remotely, or shared with anyone.

Photos and Videos

Photos and video clips you capture are saved to your device's photo library only when you choose to save them. They stay on your device — MirrorBooth never uploads your media. Sharing a photo or video sends it only where you choose in your device's share sheet.

Microphone

Microphone access is requested only for recording video clips with sound and for the experimental video-call feature. Audio is never recorded or transmitted without your explicit action.

Video Calls

If you use the video-call feature, your camera and microphone streams are transmitted directly to the other participant using peer-to-peer WebRTC. A signaling server is used only to establish the connection; calls are not recorded, stored, or monitored. As with any peer-to-peer call, the network (IP) addresses of both devices are exchanged so they can connect; a public STUN server operated by Google helps the devices discover them. Nothing about the call is kept after it ends.

Purchases

Optional filter collections are sold as one-time in-app purchases through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never receive your payment details or account information. The app only remembers on your device which collections you own.

Anonymous Usage Statistics

What is sent. Short records of moments in the app: the app was opened or went to the background, which screen was shown and for how many seconds (camera, video playback, purchase sheet), which filter and filter collection was picked, that a feature was used for the first time in a session (for example video recording, rotating the picture or switching the camera), that saving or sharing a photo or video started and how it ended (saved, cancelled, failed, no gallery access) and how many seconds it took, that a purchase started and how it ended, that a link opened the app, and that an error message was shown (its type only). Each record also carries the app version, platform (for example iOS or Android), app language, whether you own any paid collection, and two coarse bands: roughly how many times the app has been used on this device (1, 2, 3–5, 6–10, 11+) and roughly how many days ago that began (0, 1, 2–6, 7–29, 30+). On Android, the first record of a new installation may include the name of the campaign (for example a QR code) that led to the installation, as Google Play reports it. The full list is published in the app's source repository (docs/telemetry.md).

Never your photos. The statistics contain nothing about what the camera sees or what you capture: no image, no video frame, no sound, no face data, no file name, no location of a photo.

Why. To see what new users do after the first launch, where they stop, which filters and features are used, and where saving, sharing or buying fails, so we can fix it.

No identifiers. The statistics contain no name, no email, no account, no device or advertising identifier, no exact device model and no text you type. Each session gets a random number that exists only while the app runs, so two sessions cannot be linked to each other or to you. The statistics are not used for advertising or tracking and are not sold.

Who receives them, and where. The statistics are received and stored by the developer on our own server in the EU (Czech Republic). No one else receives them. Sending them, like any internet connection, reveals your IP address to our server; it is used only to work out your country and is not stored or logged. Your country is the only location information kept.

How long. Statistics are kept for 12 months and then deleted.

Offline. The app works fully offline. Without a connection, up to 500 recent records wait on your device and are sent later; older ones are discarded.

How to turn them off. Tap the gear button in the top right corner of the camera screen and switch off Anonymous usage statistics (the label is translated into your language). This takes effect immediately: records waiting on your device are deleted and nothing more is sent, until you turn it back on.

Data We Do Not Collect

MirrorBooth does not collect:

— Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, account)

— Your photos, videos, sound or anything the camera sees

— Precise location (only the country, as above)

— Device identifiers, the advertising ID or the exact device model

— Contacts, files or anything outside the app

Local Storage

The app remembers on your device which collections you own and a few counters for the rating prompt (standard platform storage, SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults). We never receive them. The statistics keep two small numbers there, the day measuring began and a session counter, from which the bands above are worked out on your device.

Third-Party Services

MirrorBooth contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The anonymous statistics above go only to our own server.

Children

MirrorBooth is not directed at children under 13 and is not offered in the App Store Kids category or Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: the anonymous statistics described above contain nothing that identifies a person, and they can be turned off in the app.
